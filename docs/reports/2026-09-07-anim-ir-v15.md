# anim-ir v15: labels in Japanese — the width estimate against the browser (2026-09-07)

## Question

Every width estimate in `vlmkit-anim` counted characters and multiplied by 0.6
em (0.55 in the layout geometry). Fourteen rounds of writers wrote English, so
nobody noticed that a CJK glyph is not 0.6 em wide. The question this round
asks is the one v13 asked of lines: **is `layout`'s green true for a Japanese
figure?** And then, after the estimate is fixed: can a writer produce a
Japanese figure from the guide alone, and does anything about Japanese text
still catch them?

## The estimate against Chromium

Measured with `getComputedTextLength` in headless Chromium at 14px with the
renderer's own font stack (`system-ui, sans-serif`; IPAGothic served the CJK
glyphs):

| text | measured px | per glyph (em) | old estimate | new estimate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `core` | 30.3 | 0.54 | 33.6 | 33.6 |
| `Application services` | 140.1 | 0.50 | 176.4 | 176.4 |
| `MEASUREMENT` | 108.2 | 0.70 | 92.4 | 107.8 |
| `0123456789` | 89.1 | 0.64 | 84.0 | 98.0 |
| 在庫 | 28.0 | **1.00** | 16.8 | 28.0 |
| 決済サービス | 84.0 | **1.00** | 50.4 | 84.0 |
| 注文を記録する | 98.0 | **1.00** | 58.8 | 98.0 |
| 「禁止」 | 56.0 | **1.00** | 33.6 | 56.0 |
| 한국어 모듈 | 74.5 | 0.89 | 50.4 | 78.4 |
| Ａ Ｂ Ｃ | 50.9 | 0.73 | 42.0 | 50.4 |
| → / ✗ | 11.7 | 0.84 | 8.4 | 11.9 |
| 🚀 deploy | 68.6 | 0.61 | 75.6 | 72.8 |

Every CJK glyph is exactly one em. The old estimate placed a Japanese label at
60% of its width; capitals and digits were 10–15% under too; lowercase Latin
was over-estimated, which is the safe direction and why the English rounds
never saw it.

`src/text-width.ts` now decides the width once, by Unicode class: East Asian
Wide and Fullwidth ranges and emoji at 1 em, arrows / math / dingbats at 0.85,
capitals and digits at the Latin em plus a tenth, combining marks and
zero-width joiners at 0, Latin at the em the caller chooses (0.6 in the
compiler, 0.55 in the geometry). `labelWidth`, the geometry's text box,
`wrapText`, `wrapCaption` and the chart legend read it. Wrapping breaks at
spaces where there are any, between glyphs for spaceless CJK, and never inside
a single Latin word. `text-width.test.ts` pins the estimate to the
measurements above: never under by more than 4%, never over by more than a
third.

## The picture, before and after

The fixture `modules-checkout-ja.json` is the request-walk module map with
Japanese labels. Rendered by the compiler on `main` and loaded into Chromium,
six of its eight boxed labels were wider than their box — 「在庫管理サービス」
and 「メッセージキュー」 by 22px each, 「ストアフロント」 by 17px — and the
callout text ran past both ends of its box. `layout` reported **0 issues** on
that timeline. After the fix: 0 of 8 labels wider than their box, `layout`
still 0 issues, and this time the two agree.

That truth check — the SVG in Chromium, every `<text>` measured against the
`<rect>` it shares a group with — is the measurement the geometry lacked. It
is a scratch script this round; whether it belongs in the tool as
`layout --measure` is a question below.

## What the wider digits uncovered

Making digits 0.7 em instead of 0.6 pushed the heap fixture's value tokens
over the `crossed` threshold: `"5" has a line through it — 6px inside the text`
in four frames. The tree edge runs centre to centre *under* the slot circles,
and the value sits *on* the circle; nothing is visibly crossed, the geometry
simply did not know that a filled shape between a stroke and a text hides the
stroke. It does now: the part of a stroke inside a fill drawn after the stroke
and before the text is subtracted before the threshold. The heap fixture is
clean again and the other seventeen fixtures were unchanged.

> ia: "The tool sized boxes automatically for the glyphs; no hand-positioning
> was needed. … The 68-character explanation wrapped correctly across two
> lines within its box."

> ib: "Circle radii scale with label length exactly as the guide promises ('a

The Chromium truth check agrees: across the three writers' figures, zero of
nineteen boxed labels are wider than their box. What did cost rounds was
placement, and both cases were made visible by Japanese labels being wider
than the English ones every earlier round had used.

### Friction, and what it changed

**ib — a transition through a state, fixed by reordering the list.**

> "The one real friction was topological, not linguistic: two states
> (`preparing`, `paid`) sharing an event name (`キャンセル要求`) to the same
> target (`refunded`) caused a label/box collision that neither `layout: 'tb'`
> nor `layout: 'circle'` fixed by itself; reordering the `states` array did.
> The guide never states that list order affects layering/tie-breaking in
> `lr`/`tb` — I inferred it from behavior, not from a documented rule."

> "The `check` hint for the collision ('move one of them, shorten the text, or
> widen the canvas') didn't mention reordering or switching layout, which were
> the two things that actually worked."

Reproduced with ib's states in the brief's order: 支払い完了 → 返金済み ran
straight through 出荷準備中 (`layout`: 18 overlaps, 9 crossed over nine
frames). The diagram compiler had routed edges around bystander boxes since
v13; the state-machine compiler had not. The router now lives in
`compile/route.ts` and both use it: the transition bends around the state, its
label takes a spot no state or earlier label holds (the two キャンセル要求
labels into 返金済み had landed on each other), and the token follows the
bend. ib's briefed order compiles clean with no reordering. The router also
learned to prefer the shorter detour and, near a tie, the side the destination
lies on — the first bend went left of 出荷準備中 and swung back across the
発送 arrow. The guide's `layout` row now says what ib inferred, and the hint
names both levers.

> ib: "`layout: 'circle'` regressing so badly on a mostly-linear-with-two-
> branches graph was surprising given the guide lists it as a peer option with
> no caveat about branch count."

The row says so now.

**ic — a relation's label past the left edge.**

> "初版: `relate` ラベル「冪等キー: A1（同一）」は 40px 越境 → 修正: 「キー A1
> （同一）」に短縮 → 9px 越境 → 最終版: 「A1（同一）」に短縮 → ✓"

The relation ran beside the leftmost lane, and every label candidate was on
its far side — off the canvas. The label now also tries the near side and both
ends of the line; ic's original label sits between the line and the lane with
zero issues. ic shortened the label three times to fit; the brief's wording
survives now.

**ia — nothing.** First check green with the sheet, first `layout` clean,
callout wrapped, no friction reported.

### What the round did not settle

- The Chromium truth check (`getComputedTextLength` against the box the text
  shares a group with) stays a scratch script. It is the measurement that
  found this round's defect and the one thing that can tell when the estimate
  drifts again; `layout --measure` would make it a verb. Left for the next
  round that touches the geometry.
- Lowercase Latin is still estimated at 0.6 em against a measured 0.50–0.55,
  a deliberate over-estimate. Tightening it would move every English layout by
  a few pixels and regenerate every sample for no defect found.
- ib wanted `check` to suggest reordering; it now does, but the compiler fix
  makes that advice rarer to need. Whether any collision remains that only a
  reorder can fix is unmeasured.
