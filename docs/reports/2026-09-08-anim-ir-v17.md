# anim-ir v17: where an annotation lands — growth on every side, pointers round boxes, and a warning when `side` is not honoured (2026-09-08)

## Question

Two placement complaints had survived from the v13/v14 still-figure rounds:

> fd (v13): a callout on a module at the top-left corner had nowhere on the
> canvas — every spot covered a text or ran through an edge — and the canvas
> could only grow at the bottom.

> hc (v14): a wrapped callout's pointer crossed the `events` module on its way
> to the edge it was about.

Both are about the same thing: `side` was a preference the compiler honoured
only when the canvas as drawn already had the room, and a pointer was a
straight line whatever lay under it. This round makes the canvas grow on the
side the writer asked for, routes the pointer, and — after three writers had
measured their SVGs by hand to find out where their notes went — makes
`check` say so when a stated side was not honoured.

## What was added

- **The canvas grows on any side.** The annotation layer keeps bounds
  (`x0/y0/x1/y1`) that include room asked for above and to the left, and the
  Builder shifts every root node, every position keyframe of one, and a
  matrix's slot table by that room when it builds the timeline. A stated
  `side` whose spot is clear but lies just past the edge wins over a clear
  spot on another side, and the canvas grows there (bounded at 120px — a note
  a whole picture away is not beside anything). Growth on a side other than
  the asked one stays the last resort, after every side on the canvas.
- **A callout's pointer goes round labelled boxes and free-standing labels**
  on the shared router (`compile/route.ts`) — and only when the detour
  crosses less than the straight line did: the first version bent a matrix
  pointer round two cells and through the row letters "B" and "C" instead.
- **A callout on a bent edge points at the middle of the line** (by length),
  not at the edge's first end. hc's pointer had been routed round the very
  module the edge left because the target sat on that module's corner.
- **`check` reports a stated `side` that was not honoured** (added from this
  round's friction, below): `⚠ sequence[2].callout.side: the callout at
  "diagnostics" asked for \`above\` and landed \`below\`: a line runs through
  that spot`, with the hint to ask for the landed side (or drop `side`) or
  make room. The default side is a preference and moves silently.

### Before / after on the corpus

`layout` over the 20 package fixtures and 85 scenario scenes: **no issue count
changed** — the growth only enters when nothing on the canvas is clear, and the
routed pointer is kept only when it crosses less. Where behaviour did change:

| scene | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| `matrix-vector-clock`, `value` asked `right` of row A | below the grid | beside row A; canvas +39px right |
| `matrix-vector-clock`, callout on `0,0` | in the panel at the right, a 150px pointer across the row | below the target, a short straight pointer |
| hc, callout on `events->handlers` | pointer into the `events` box (the edge's first end) | pointer to the edge's middle, bent round `config` |
| a `left` callout on the top module of a chain (test) | placed above | left; canvas +57px, every node shifted right by 57 |

## The writers

Two briefs on shapes the guide's examples do not use (the v16 caveat):
`compiler-pipeline-still` — seven modules, four groups, a forbidden and a
highlighted dependency, and three callouts each **asked for on a named side**
(left of `lexer`, right of `codegen`, above `diagnostics`) — with its fact
sheet; and `matrix-lcs-readouts` — a 3×3 DP table with a callout `above`
the first cell, two readouts `right` of rows and a callout `left` of the last
cell. Each writer records, per annotation, the side asked and the side it
landed on, read off the SVG.

| agent | model | brief | first `check` | rounds | `layout` | asked = landed | hand-typed | bytes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ka | sonnet | compiler | 0 ✗ 0 ⚠ | 1 | clean | 2 of 3 (`above` diagnostics → below) | 0 | 1232 |
| kb | haiku | compiler | 0 ✗ 0 ⚠ | 1 | clean | 2 of 3 — **reported 3 of 3** | 0 | 1204 |
| kc | sonnet | LCS | 0 ✗ 0 ⚠ | 1 | clean | 2 of 4 (one callout without `side`, one `left` → below) | 0 | 1237 |

Every scene was green on the first `check`. Both `left` and `right` asks
landed as asked with the canvas grown on that side (ka: 844×612 → 937×644;
kc: 320 → 375 wide). The two that did not:

- `above diagnostics` (ka, kb): `diagnostics` is at the bottom of the column
  under `typeck`, `lower`, `codegen`, and the edges into it run exactly where
  the note would go. Below is the honest answer; the picture is right.
- `left of 2,2` (kc): the neighbour cell is there, and one box further left
  the pointer would run through two cells and a row letter; `right` — the
  fallback in isolation — was taken by the row-B readout, so it went below.

kb's log says all three callouts landed where asked. Two did; the third is
below. The smaller model's reading of its own SVG was wrong, which is the
strongest argument for the tool saying it: with the warning added after the
round, `check` on kb's unchanged scene reports the substitution in one line.

### Friction, verbatim

> ka: "neither `check` nor `layout` flagged this (both only check structural
> facts / collision-freedom, not 'did the requested side survive'). … I
> would have wanted either (a) a `⚠` from `check`/`layout` naming 'asked side
> left, landed side right' the same way a misspelt anchor gets named, or (b)
> a scene-level option like 'reserve a lane on this side even if it costs a
> layer'."

> kc: "`side` reads as a request in the doc's prose … but is closer to an
> initial preference the engine is free to override with no signal back to
> the writer *that* it did — `check` and `layout` were both fully green on a
> scene where 2 of my 4 explicit side choices were silently overridden. … A
> warning — 'callout `0,0` asked for `side: above`, placed `below`' — would
> have told me this in the terminal instead of a text editor."

> kc: "an `ms: 0` annotation is invisible for the *entire* folded beat and
> only appears at that beat's end (the next step's start) … if a
> contact-sheet tool grabs the frame at the step's start time, which is what
> `render --step N` does, the thing the caption just said happened is not
> actually drawn yet."

> kc: "I could not find a way to ask 'prefer right, but stack under the value
> readout instead of jumping to the opposite edge of the canvas' — the side
> vocabulary is four cardinal choices with an opaque search behind them."

> kc: "`check`'s own byte count for the scene ('scene 1012 B') does not match
> `wc -c scene.json` (1237 B) … presumably … its own canonicalized/minified
> form, but the report doesn't say so."

> kb: "The initial attempt had all three callouts with the default `id="main"`,
> which caused them to replace each other instead of coexisting. This was
> discovered by reading the SVG where the first two callouts had
> `opacity="0"`."

> ka: "the brief's own success bar (`check --expect` clean + `layout` clean +
> notes visible) is fully satisfied by a scene that gets a requested side
> *wrong*."

### What changed from it

- The placement warning above (ka's (a), kc's ask), `Placement` notes carried
  in `Timeline.meta.placements` and read by `check`. The `matrix-vector-clock`
  test scene that asked `above` under a title now leaves `side` unset, which
  is what the warning tells a writer to do when below reads fine.
- The guide's placement paragraph says a stated side that cannot be honoured
  is a warning and the default side is not; the `ms: 0` paragraph says "the
  moment" is the beat's end and a frame at the step's start does not show it.
- `check` prints `scene 1012 B (minified)`.

### What the round did not settle

- **ka's (b), kc's "near here"**: a stronger ask than `side` — "reserve a lane
  on this side even at the cost of a layer", or "the closest free spot in any
  direction". Not added: the four sides plus a truthful warning is a contract
  a writer can act on, and no scene in the corpus has needed more. The first
  brief that does should decide it.
- **The default side moves silently.** kc's first callout had no `side` and
  went below the table with nothing said. A default is not an ask, but a
  writer who reads "callout … `side` default `above`" may hear one; the guide
  now says which it is.
- **Growth is bounded at 120px.** A callout wider than that past the edge
  gets no growth and falls to another side, with the warning naming the
  distance. Whether a wider note should widen the canvas anyway is unmeasured.
- **Leader-line labels** (this round's title had them) were not built: no
  writer since v12 has asked for a label to leave its edge, and the halo has
  covered every label-on-line case in the corpus.
- **Identity**: kb's three callouts replaced each other until they had ids —
  the guide's "Identity" paragraph says so, and `check`'s
  `annotations: 3 drawn, 1 on screen at the end` said so, but only a writer
  who knows what the count should be reads it that way.
