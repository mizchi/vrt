# anim-ir v19: two kinds for the shapes people draw — `flowchart` and `gantt` (2026-09-08)

## Question

Sixteen kinds and no flowchart. The user's list of what a general
diagramming-and-animation tool still lacked put new kinds fifth: sequence
diagrams, flowcharts, timelines / Gantt charts, nested boxes. This round takes
the two that are most often drawn by hand and least well served by what exists
— a `graph` has no decision diamond and no answer on an edge; a `chart` has no
time axis with bars on it — and asks three writers to draw with them from the
guide alone.

## What was added

- **`kind: flowchart`.** Nodes are `process` (a box), `decision` (a diamond),
  `terminal` (a pill: start, end, give up) or `io` (a slanted box); edges carry
  a decision's answer as `label`; `walk` lists the nodes visited after `start`,
  every hop an edge, and a token follows it — captioned `2xx?: no → tries < 3?`
  out of a decision. Nodes are layered by distance from `start` (not by the
  longest path, which had put the loop body at the bottom of the page); a loop
  back to an earlier node runs round the outside of the chart, out of the
  node's side and back in at the target's, stepping off any node in the way.
  The check warns about a decision with one way out, an unlabelled way out, a
  node the walk never reaches and a walk that stops mid-flow; `check --expect`
  reads `nodes`, `edges` (`"a->b:yes"`), `visited` and `end`.
- **`kind: gantt`.** Tasks are bars on a unit axis (`start` / `end` in days,
  weeks, sprints — a number line, not a calendar), grouped into `lane` bands,
  with `after` dependency arrows, `milestone` diamonds and an `owner` drawn in
  the bar. `ops` move a time cursor (`advance` — bars fill as it passes and the
  step says `day 3: Build starts; Design finishes`), `slip` a task's dates (with
  `cascade`, every dependent that would now start too early moves with it, and
  the step names them), or set a `status` (`late` / `blocked` / `done`). The
  check warns when a task starts before something it depends on ends and when
  the cursor never reaches a task's end; it errors when time runs backwards.

Both flowchart writers were green on the first write — sheet, layout and
narration — and both by the smaller and the larger model:

> ma: "the four shapes are each given in one clause with a concrete reason to
> pick them ('a pill: start, end, give up' told me immediately which two nodes
> in a 10-node brief get `terminal`) … translating the fact sheet into a scene
> was mechanical, not a puzzle. … The two loop-backs in the brief are exactly
> the case the guide calls out."

> mb: "Every check line was actionable and the scene required zero edits."

mc's five rounds were one problem: a `callout` on the wiring task, placed
clear at its beat, had the cursor's label `t = 9` move under its pointer two
beats later —

> mc: "the suggested remedies (side, shorter label, different anchor) didn't
> apply, since the real conflict was temporal (a permanent callout vs. a
> moving cursor label), not spatial."

— and mc's fix was `{"callout": null, "ms": 0}` before that beat. The
compiler's fix is that the cursor label now rides the cursor line just under
the axis, out of the band annotations land in; mc's scene without the `null`
is clean. The v17 placement warning was, in mc's words, "the most useful line
in the session".

### Friction, verbatim

> mc: "Biggest gap: no cascading slip — `slip` documents that 'dependents do
> not move, slip them too,' but there's no shorthand for 'push everything
> downstream by the same amount' … Had to hand-derive and hand-write three
> separate `slip` ops (wire, test, demo) for one root cause."

> mc: "No field for who is assigned to a task — nothing reads naturally as a
> per-bar label/owner."

> mc: "No second axis — … a burn-down line alongside the bars isn't
> expressible."

> mc: "the cursor label only ever shows the raw unit value ('t = 9'), no
> custom per-tick caption (e.g. real calendar dates)."

> ma: "the expect schema describes `visited` only as 'the walk from start' with
> no explicit statement of whether it includes the start node."

### What changed from it

- `slip` takes `cascade: true`; the fixture, guide and sheet use it, and the
  step names what moved (`Build slips: 3–8 → 3–9; QA, Ship move with it`).
- `owner` on a task, drawn inside the bar or after it when the bar is short.
- The cursor label rides the cursor under the axis; rows start lower.
- The annotation-placement hint names the temporal case: when the thing in the
  way moves there later, hide the annotation with `null` before that beat.
- The sheet and the guide say `visited` starts with `start`.

### What the round did not settle

- **A second axis** (mc): a burn-down line over the bars is a `compose` of a
  `gantt` and a `chart`, not one picture. Whether it should be one waits for a
  brief that needs it.
- **Calendar labels** on the axis: `unit` is a word and ticks are numbers by
  design; a `labels` map for ticks is cheap and not asked for twice yet.
- **A moving element under a standing annotation** is now handled for this
  kind's cursor by moving the cursor label, not in general: a token or a
  cursor in any kind can still walk under a callout placed at an earlier beat.
  `layout` reports it; the placement does not yet look ahead.
- The remaining kinds from the list — a sequence diagram (a `distributed`
  preset with activation bars) and nested boxes (groups inside groups) — are
  the next round.
